About Joni Medtech Supply

Welcome to Joni Medtech Supply, your trusted online destination for medical laboratory supplies, consumables, diagnostic products, and laboratory equipment.

We are committed to making essential laboratory and healthcare products more accessible to medical laboratories, healthcare facilities, diagnostic centres, research institutions, students, and other professionals in the healthcare sector.

Our product range includes laboratory reagents, diagnostic test kits, blood collection supplies, personal protective equipment, laboratory consumables, microscopes, centrifuges, hematology analysers, chemistry analysers, and other laboratory equipment.

At Joni Medtech Supply, we understand that reliable laboratory supplies are important for efficient diagnostic and research activities. Our goal is to provide customers with a convenient platform where they can browse products, compare prices, and make enquiries about products that meet their specific needs.

Our Mission

Our mission is to improve access to laboratory supplies and healthcare-related products by providing a convenient, reliable, and customer-focused online shopping experience.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted medical and laboratory supply provider in Ghana and, in the future, expand our reach to serve customers across Africa.
